# Paper AI

Paper AI is An AI-native research platform for investment banks, hedge funds, private equity, venture capital, family offices, and institutional investors.

Unlike generic AI chatbots, the platform combines proprietary financial data, internal firm knowledge, and live market intelligence into a single agent capable of performing institutional-grade research.

---

## Vision

Build the Bloomberg Terminal for AI agents.

One platform where financial professionals can search, analyze, model, and automate every research workflow using natural language.

---

## Core Value

Instead of searching documents, users search knowledge.

The platform understands companies, funds, executives, markets, filings, transactions, financial metrics, relationships, and internal research.
